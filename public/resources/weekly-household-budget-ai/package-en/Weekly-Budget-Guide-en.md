# Weekly Household Budget with AI

A practical guide for organizing a limited weekly household amount without letting AI make financial decisions for you.

## Real example: 300 TND for one week

A family of two adults and one child has 300 TND for food, cleaning, transport, and emergencies. The workflow helps them ask the right questions, divide the amount, create a priority shopping list, track actual spending, and adjust when prices change.

## Workflow stages

1. **Budget input:** enter the amount, family size, days, existing supplies, fixed costs, and emergency reserve.
2. **Clarify:** ask what is missing instead of guessing prices or needs.
3. **Allocate:** split the amount into essential food, fresh items, protein, cleaning, transport, emergency reserve, and flexible spending.
4. **Shop:** create priority levels: essential, useful, and deferrable.
5. **Track:** record actual prices and calculate the remaining balance.
6. **Review:** decide what to postpone or replace when the budget changes.

## Where to use this workflow

- **Manual with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini:** paste the prompt, answer the questions, then review the proposed plan.
- **Semi-automatic with Google Sheets:** record purchases and ask AI to analyze the remaining balance.
- **Automated with Make or n8n:** send a form or spreadsheet update to an AI step, then route the result to Notion, Sheets, or a reminder draft.

AI organizes options and performs calculations. A person confirms prices, priorities, purchases, and any action involving money.

## Safety boundary

This is an organization template, not financial advice. Do not share card numbers, bank credentials, or sensitive identity data. Verify prices and availability locally.
