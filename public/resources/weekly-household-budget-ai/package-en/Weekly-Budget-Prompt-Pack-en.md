# Weekly Budget Prompt Pack

## Intake prompt
I want help organizing my weekly household budget.
Budget: [amount and currency]
People: [adults / children]
Days: [number]
Already at home: [items]
Fixed costs this week: [transport, medicine, bills]
Emergency reserve: [amount]

Ask me the missing questions first. Then create: (1) a category allocation, (2) an essential shopping list, (3) items that can wait, (4) a remaining-balance tracker, and (5) a cheaper alternative plan. Do not invent prices or make purchases.

## Actual-spending prompt
Here is my real shopping list and the prices paid: [paste rows]. Compare it with the original plan. Show spent, remaining, reserve protected, categories over budget, and what can be postponed.

## Change scenario
The available amount changed from [old] to [new]. Recalculate the plan while protecting food essentials, transport needs, and the emergency reserve. Explain every trade-off.
