# Weekly Budget Quality & Safety Checklist

- [ ] The available amount and currency are explicit.
- [ ] Household size and number of days are explicit.
- [ ] Existing supplies are separated from new purchases.
- [ ] Fixed costs are listed before optional spending.
- [ ] An emergency reserve is protected.
- [ ] AI did not invent prices, discounts, or availability.
- [ ] Essential items are separated from deferrable items.
- [ ] Actual prices are checked by a person.
- [ ] No card, bank, or identity credentials were shared.
- [ ] The final plan is approved by the person managing the money.
