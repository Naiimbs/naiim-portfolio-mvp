# AI Workflow Quality & Safety Checklist

- [ ] Outcome and success metric are explicit.
- [ ] Owner and escalation path are named.
- [ ] Inputs, source freshness, and permissions are defined.
- [ ] Every AI task has a narrow scope and structured output.
- [ ] Missing, conflicting, duplicate, and adversarial inputs are tested.
- [ ] Confidence thresholds and human approval gates are configured.
- [ ] Personal, financial, legal, medical, hiring, and deletion actions receive human review.
- [ ] Logs, rate limits, access controls, retention, and rollback exist.
- [ ] Errors fail safely and do not silently trigger external actions.
- [ ] ROI is measured together with quality and correction rate.
- [ ] Evaluation cases are versioned and reviewed after changes.
