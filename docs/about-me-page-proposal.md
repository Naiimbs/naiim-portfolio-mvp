# About Me Page — Content & Structure Proposal

Website: https://naiimbsili.com/

## Objective

The About page should go beyond a traditional CV. It should explain who I am, how I think, and what I bring to teams and digital products.

My professional journey connects mechanical design and fabrication, graphic design, UX/UI, low-code implementation, AI product building, and automation.

The page should feel personal, credible, editorial, and consistent with my existing website identity.

## 1. Hero — Beyond a Job Title

### Headline

**I DESIGN. I BUILD. I EXPERIMENT.**

### Supporting text

I turn complex problems into usable digital products through product design, AI, low-code, and automation.

### Suggested elements

- A suitable personal portrait.
- A clear call to action: **Explore My Work**.
- A second call to action: **Let's Talk**.
- A small **Currently Building** indicator highlighting a current project or experiment.

Keep the first screen concise and make the professional positioning clear immediately.

## 2. My Journey — From Mechanical Design to AI

This is the signature section of the page. Present my professional evolution as a visual, interactive timeline rather than a conventional CV list.

### Suggested stages

1. **Mechanical Design & Fabrication**  
   Learning how things are designed, made, and improved; developing a practical approach to problem-solving.

2. **Graphic & Visual Design**  
   Turning ideas into visual communication and developing a strong sense of composition and clarity.

3. **UX/UI & Product Design**  
   Understanding user needs and designing useful, intuitive digital experiences.

4. **Low-Code & Implementation**  
   Connecting design decisions to real application behavior and helping bridge the gap between design and production.

5. **AI Product Building & Automation**  
   Experimenting with AI workflows, integrations, agents, and automation to build more connected digital experiences.

For each stage, consider including a period or year only when verified, a relevant image or artifact, a short explanation, and one lesson learned.

The timeline should communicate how my way of thinking evolved, not simply list job titles.

## 3. How I Think — My Philosophy

Explain how I approach problems, beyond the tools I use.

### Understand before designing

Understand the real problem and its context before deciding what to build.

### Design with implementation in mind

A design should consider real technical constraints and preserve usability when it reaches production.

### Experiment to learn

Test assumptions, build prototypes, learn from results, and improve the solution rather than assuming the first idea is the best.

### Signature statement

**I think. I design. I experiment.**

Present expertise in clear groups rather than an oversized list of skills.

## 5. Selected Work — Proof, Not Claims

Show three or four projects that demonstrate different aspects of my work.

Potential candidates:

- **WINNI** — A QR/NFC concept for lost-item identification.
- **Naim Copilot** — An AI assistant and workflow experimentation project.
- **Enterprise Product Design** — A case study from banking or government work, respecting client confidentiality.
- **AI & Automation Lab** — A real workflow explained through a diagram and its demonstrated outcome.

For each project, clarify:

- The problem or opportunity.
- My specific role and contribution.
- The approach and key decisions.
- The result, using verified evidence only.
- Whether it is a professional project, a personal project, a concept, or an experiment.

Do not invent metrics, outcomes, users, or business impact. Respect confidentiality and avoid exposing restricted client information.

## 6. Beyond the Portfolio — What I Share

Show that I also document my learning and share knowledge.

Suggested content:

- Two or three recent articles from the blog.
- Selected experiments from the lab.
- Training, workshops, or educational resources.
- Verified teaching and training figures, where relevant and clearly contextualized.

Potential proof points, if accurate and appropriate for publication:

- **1,200+ training hours**
- **300+ learners**

Link each item to its full page or resource. Keep this section concise.

## 7. Currently Building

Add a small section that can be updated as my priorities evolve.

Feature one current project or experiment, with:

- Project name.
- One-sentence explanation.
- Current stage, accurately described.
- Link to learn more, if available.

Avoid presenting a concept or experiment as a launched product.

## 8. Beyond Work — The Person Behind the Products

Include a short personal note about the curiosity that connects the different stages of my journey: understanding how things work, making ideas tangible, solving practical problems, and sharing what I learn.

Keep this authentic and relevant to my professional identity. It should add a human dimension without turning the page into a long autobiography.

## 9. Let's Build Something Meaningful

I'm interested in meaningful product challenges where design, technology, and AI come together.

### Calls to action

- **Explore My Work**
- **Let's Talk**

Make the next step clear and easy to find.

## Visual & UX Direction

- Follow the existing identity and design system of naiimbsili.com.
- Reuse the website's established colors, typography, spacing, components, and motion patterns.
- Prefer strong storytelling, clear hierarchy, and meaningful visual artifacts over decorative effects.
- Make the page responsive and accessible.
- Avoid turning every section into a card; vary the composition while maintaining consistency.
- Use real project artifacts and personal images where available.
- Keep animations purposeful and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Ensure Arabic or multilingual content, if used, is rendered with the correct language and text direction.

## Content Accuracy Rules

- Verify dates, roles, project details, and metrics before publishing.
- Distinguish professional experience from personal projects, concepts, and experiments.
- Do not disclose confidential client information.
- Do not invent results, testimonials, responsibilities, or technical ownership.
- Prefer specific evidence and clear explanations over inflated claims.

## Core Recommendation

Make **My Journey** the signature of the page. The progression from mechanical design and fabrication to visual design, UX/UI, low-code implementation, and AI product building gives the professional positioning a clear story.

The About page should help visitors understand not only which tools I use, but how I think, how I work, and why I approach digital products the way I do.
